Robot introduction

Fracture’s robot is designed and built by the team each FTC season. It combines mechanical subsystems, electrical wiring, and software to complete the game challenge. The current season’s robot is in the design and prototyping phase.

Subsystems, mechanisms, and specifications below are placeholders pending the season’s design review. They will be replaced with real technical information as the robot is built.
