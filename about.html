Team introduction

Fracture is a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team based in Round Rock, Texas, competing as team 37687. Each season we design, fabricate, program, and operate a robot to meet the FTC game challenge. The team is built around mechanical engineering, software development, electrical systems, and outreach work.

Every member contributes to design, fabrication, programming, outreach, or competition operations. Collaboration and clear communication are part of every build session.

01 / PROCESS HOW WE WORK

How we build

Design

We start each season by analyzing the game rules, brainstorming mechanisms, and creating CAD models before any parts are cut.

Fabricate

Parts are machined, assembled, and iterated in the workshop. We test mechanisms early and revise based on what we learn.

Program

Autonomous and driver-control code is written, tested on the field, and refined through competition and practice.

Document

Engineering notebooks, CAD files, and code are maintained throughout the season so the team can build on its work year over year.

Teamwork

Fracture runs as a team. Members share responsibility for design, fabrication, programming, outreach, and competition operations. No one person owns the robot — it is built together, session by session.

Collaboration

Design decisions are discussed as a group. Build tasks are shared across members. Competition roles are assigned so everyone has a clear responsibility on event day.

01 / GOALS THIS SEASON

Goals

What we’re working toward

We compete in FTC qualification tournaments each season, aim to advance through the tournament structure, and use each event to improve the robot and the team. Along the way, we promote STEM and robotics in our school and community.

Long-term, the team wants to grow its membership, strengthen its engineering documentation, and increase its outreach presence in Round Rock and the surrounding area.
